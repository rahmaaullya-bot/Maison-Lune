Our Story

Maison Lune is a soft luxury fashion brand inspired by timeless femininity and delicate elegance. Founded as a dream of creating pieces that feel gentle yet sophisticated, Maison Lune brings together pastel tones, graceful silhouettes, and refined details to create a dreamy wardrobe for modern women.

Each collection is carefully curated to reflect softness and confidence, allowing every wearer to express elegance effortlessly. From flowing dresses to charming knitwear, Maison Lune is designed to accompany your most beautiful moments.
